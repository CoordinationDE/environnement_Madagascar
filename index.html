About This Website

In an effort to facilitate collaboration and improve transparency, this platform was created to visualize the intervention zones of development projects working within a shared geographical and thematic context, at the moment still limited to the Regions DIANA and Boeny.
All participating projects operate in the green sector of Madagascar — with focus on agriculture, food security, natural resource management, environmental restoration, and/or reforestation. By making this information openly accessible, the platform aims to help identify synergies and overlaps between projects, foster information exchange, and ultimately enhance the collective impact of individual initiatives.
In addition, the technical team of the PADDI project has integrated monthly fire data into the system. This crucial information is available both on the Fire Dashboard and as a geospatial layer within the Interactive Map, providing valuable insights into fire dynamics across the regions DIANA and Boeny.
